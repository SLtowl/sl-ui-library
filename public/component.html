Make it yours

Take the component

Choose Pair, Save or Cancel, then download the zip. Unpack it and open index.html. The example works offline, with no dependencies.

Connect your action

In example.js, connect onSave to your async save function and onCancel to your close or discard action. Pass the supplied signal to abortable requests.

The button shows Saved when your promise resolves. Cancel aborts the pending operation. A failed save is announced to assistive technology; handle visible error messaging in your project.

The preview uses a 1.9-second simulated request in preview.js; it sends no data. Remove previewDuration when connecting a real request. There is no fixed loading delay in the component.

Adjust the look

Edit the .button-kit variables in buttons.css for colors, sizing and timing. The included Instrument Sans font is covered by OFL.txt. Keyboard focus and reduced-motion preferences are supported.
